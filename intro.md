# DATA 88E Materials

Welcome to **DATA 88E: Economic Models** lecture and lab materials.

This book contains Jupyter notebooks for all three parts of the edX professional certificate:

- **Part 1 - Fundamentals of Economics:** demand, supply, taxes, government intervention, and welfare
- **Part 2 - Advanced Concepts in Economics:** macroeconomic policy, utility, inequality, and development
- **Part 3 - Real-World Applications of Economics:** game theory, econometrics, environmental economics, and finance

Use the sidebar to navigate to a lecture or lab. To run notebooks interactively, open them in JupyterLab or use the "Run in Binder" link above.

For course overviews and edX enrollment, see the [DATA 88EX Professional Certificate](https://www.edx.org/certificates/professional-certificate/berkeleyx-data-88ex-economic-models-with-python) on edX.
